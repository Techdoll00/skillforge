# Daily Briefing

> Don't open twenty tabs to figure out your day. A Daily Briefing condenses your sources into one scannable, action-oriented summary.

## What it is

Daily Briefing gathers the raw material of your day — overnight notes, saved links, calendar events, open todos — and synthesizes it into a short brief that ends with the few things you should actually do today. It's the difference between "a pile of information" and "a plan for the morning."

## When to use it

- First thing in the morning, before opening your work apps
- Before a planning session or weekly review
- When your sources (feeds, notes, inbox) have outgrown what you can skim
- On a day with too many loose threads to hold in your head

## How it works

1. Gather: pull from your configured sources — notes, feeds, todos, calendar
2. Filter: drop noise; keep only what changed or matters today
3. Synthesize: group related items and summarize each in a line
4. Extract actions: end the brief with the top 3 things to do

## Example

> Sources overnight: three saved articles, a bug report, two meeting invites, and a stale todo list. The brief comes back as: "Deploy is green. The bug is likely X — fix window today. Pre-read for the 2pm review. The article about agent evals is directly relevant — 5 minutes." That's a morning, compressed.
